# RetailPulse Business Insights Report

## Project Overview

RetailPulse is a retail analytics project that analyzes customer transactions to identify sales trends, customer behavior, and business opportunities.

## Tools Used

- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- SQL (SQLite)
- Power BI
- Data Visualization

## Key Analysis Performed

### Sales Performance Analysis

Analyzed monthly revenue trends to understand sales patterns and identify high-performing periods.

### Customer Segmentation

Performed RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to identify valuable customers based on purchasing behavior.

### Geographic Analysis

Analyzed revenue contribution across different countries to identify important markets.

### Product Analysis

Identified top-performing products based on revenue contribution.

## Key Insights

- The United Kingdom generated the highest revenue contribution compared to other countries.
- A small group of high-value customers contributed significantly to overall revenue.
- Revenue patterns showed variations across different months, indicating seasonal purchasing behavior.
- RFM analysis helped identify customers with strong engagement and spending patterns.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customers.
- Develop strategies to retain loyal customers.
- Analyze seasonal trends to optimize inventory planning.
- Expand successful products in high-performing markets.
